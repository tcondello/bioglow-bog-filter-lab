# Bioglow Lake Lab — Solar Bog Filter Simulator

An interactive web simulator built for a 6th-grade **FIRST LEGO League (Bioglow)** team exploring how a **solar-powered pump** and **multi-stage bog filter** can clean an axolotl lake safely.

## What You Can Experiment With
- **Weather & Solar Power + Battery Backup**: Switch between Sunny, Cloudy, Rainy, and Night, and toggle the Battery Backup to see how stored daytime energy keeps the filter running 24/7.
- **Closed-Loop Water Circulation**: Toggle Flow Arrows to see how dirty water enters the lakebed intake, rises through the bog filter, and returns as a gentle, oxygenated stream.
- **Axolotl Intake Safety**: Compare *Fine Screen + Small Tube*, *Metal Grating*, and an *Open Pipe* to see how intake design protects adult and baby axolotls from suction.
- **Multiple Filtering Methods**: Toggle *Coarse Gravel*, *Biofilm Bacteria*, and *Wetland Plants* (plus **Microscope View**) to watch mechanical, biological, and plant-root filtration work together.
- **Ecosystem Health**: Track live Water Clarity, Ammonia, Dissolved Oxygen, and Axolotl Health as you test different flow speeds and muddy runoff events.
