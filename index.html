1. Weather & Solar
"How does weather affect our solar pump?"
★ Best Option: Solar + Battery Backup

Clouds and night drop solar power to 0%. A battery keeps the pump running 24/7 so good bacteria stay alive!

2. Stream In & Out
"Is there a stream that goes both in and out?"
★ Best Option: Closed-Loop Spillway

Pull dirty water from the bottom (where waste sinks) and return clean water over rocks at the top to splash in oxygen (O₂).

3. Axolotl Safety
"Will axolotls get sucked in?"
★ Best Option: Wide Fine Screen + Grating

A small tube speeds up suction! A wide fine mesh box spreads suction out so even baby axolotls stay safe.

4. Multiple Filters
"Why use multiple filtering methods?"
★ Best Option: All 3 Stages Together

Gravel traps mud, bacteria convert toxic ammonia into plant food, and native roots drink it up. No single layer can do all 3!

5. Lake Organisms
"Will the bog filter affect organisms?"
★ Best Option: Medium Flow + Native Plants

Helps the whole food web (clean water + O₂), while calm flow protects axolotl gills and native plants prevent invasive weeds.
